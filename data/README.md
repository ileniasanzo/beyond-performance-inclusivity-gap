# Data Governance & Privacy Compliance

## Dataset Overview
This folder is dedicated to the storage and documentation of the analytical data layer for the **Beyond Performance** Capstone Project. 

* **Master Dataset File:** `bp_wellness_master_database.csv` (1,464 long-format records).
* **Temporal Scope:** 2021 – 2026 (Post-Pandemic Macroeconomic Aggregates).
* **Geographic Coverage:** Italy (ITA) and United States of America (USA).

## Data Ethics & GDPR Compliance Statement
As a rigorous Data Analyst, data privacy and governance are embedded into the design of this pipeline:

1. **Anonymization:** The data pipeline operates exclusively on public, anonymized, and aggregated macroeconomic search index metrics.
2. **Zero PII Ingestion Risk:** No Personally Identifiable Information (PII), corporate trade secrets, or individual user data are captured, processed, or stored within this repository.
3. **Cross-Border Regulatory Alignment:** Fully compliant with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (**EU GDPR**) standards and the United States Federal Trade Commission (**US FTC**) privacy frameworks.
4. **Reproducibility:** The structural layout enforces semantic invariance, ensuring that any replication of the Python/SQL pipeline yields identical, untampered analytical results.
